The platform will eventually split into services. Phase 1 should not start as
microservices: one binary and the size of phase 1 both argue for a modular
monolith whose crates are the future boundaries. Splitting happens along crate
boundaries when load or team size demands it, not before.

Creating all the crates up front would claim scopes nothing works in. The
workspace starts with `ankhub` and `hub-store`; `hub-git`, `hub-events`,
`hub-tenancy` and `hub-auth` appear when their task is claimed.
