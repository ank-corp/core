ank is the tool with nothing to run. Coordination already speaks a standard
protocol: refs under `refs/ank/*`, updated by compare-and-swap, which the CLI
reads, pushes and reports on today. AnkHub starts by speaking that protocol, so
an unmodified `ank` pointed at it works.

Every capability the hub adds sits on top of or beside that protocol, never in
place of it. When the hub refuses something (a spoofed identity, a token out of
its scope, a ref outside the namespace), the agent sees what it already knows
how to handle: a push the remote refused, named in the verb's output.

A feature that needs a new verb to be usable is a feature that belongs in the
hub, or does not belong anywhere. The ank side of the hub (the `ank` remote
convention, the `watch` subscription, the lease clock) is decided in
`haksolot/ank` by its own ADRs, and none of them adds a verb.
