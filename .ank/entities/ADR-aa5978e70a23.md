Ratification is a human act: `ank accept`, signed with a person's SSH or GPG
key, on the default branch. The hub may hold an allowed-signers list, verify an
accept commit against it, and manage rotation and revocation (phase 2). It never
holds a signing key for ratification and never emits a signature on a person's
behalf.

A runner that publishes a detached attestation under `process:ankhub` (phase 3)
signs a proof, not a decision: a proof says a verifier ran, a ratification says
a rule binds. The constraint covers ratification.
